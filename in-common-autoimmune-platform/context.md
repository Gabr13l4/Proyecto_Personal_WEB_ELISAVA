# Context del projecte

## Informació general
- **Repositori:** \Gabr13l4/Proyecto_Personal_WEB_ELISAVA``
- **Branca consultada:** `main`
- **Tecnologies:** HTML, CSS i JavaScript natius.
- **Project:** Dual Autoimmunity Digital Platform (Celiac Disease & Hashimoto’s Thyroiditis)
- **Current Phase:** Research Synthesis, UX Conceptualization & Visual Identity (Figma)
- **Design Tools:** Figma (UI layout, color system, interactive toggle wireframes)

## Fitxers principals
### `index.html`
Defines the dark plum landing hero, the light Hashimoto’s tool section, and a third “The Intersection” explorer connecting shared symptom questions, food decisions, and medication/meal routines.

### `style.css`
Applies the responsive layouts and custom color palette (`#31263E`, `#FBF5F3`, `#FF8552`, `#8765DD`), with the first screen on deep plum and the second on warm alabaster.

### `script.js`
Handles the tri-mode hero toggle, expandable research question, second-scroll tool tabs and utilities, and third-scroll interaction prompts for symptoms, food, and medication/meal routines.

### `README.md`
Actualment conté només el títol del projecte.

## Consideracions
- The check-in, reminder schedule, and food prompts are front-end prototypes. They do not store health entries or send reminders, and food prompts do not certify products or venues as safe.
- Keep clinical choices and timing aligned with each user's care team; the interface should support organization without providing diagnosis or treatment advice.
